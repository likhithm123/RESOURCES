# History III - World History and Indian Art and Culture
World History is only in Mains GS I (1-2 questions). Art and Culture gives 4-8 Prelims questions and 1-2 Mains questions. Both reward clear concepts and a few precise examples.
## Renaissance, Reformation and Enlightenment
- **Renaissance** (14th-17th c., began in Italy): "rebirth" of classical learning; humanism, reason, individualism. Causes: fall of Constantinople (1453), printing press (Gutenberg), rich city-states, Crusades contact. Leonardo, Michelangelo, Galileo.
- **Reformation** (1517): Martin Luther's 95 Theses against sale of indulgences -> Protestantism -> weakened Church authority, rise of nation-states.
- **Enlightenment**: Locke (natural rights), Montesquieu (separation of powers), Rousseau (social contract, popular sovereignty), Voltaire (liberty of speech). These ideas flow directly into the Indian Constitution.
## Industrial Revolution (from about 1760, Britain)
- Why Britain first: coal and iron, colonies for raw material and markets, capital from trade, agricultural revolution (enclosures released labour), political stability, inventions (spinning jenny, steam engine - James Watt, power loom).
- Effects: factory system, urbanisation, working class and capitalist class, child labour, trade unions, socialism; **imperialism** for markets; in India - deindustrialisation.
## American Revolution (1775-83)
- Cause: "No taxation without representation" (Stamp Act, Tea Act; Boston Tea Party 1773). Declaration of Independence 4 July 1776 (Jefferson). Constitution 1787: first written constitution, federalism, Bill of Rights, judicial review (Marbury v. Madison 1803).
- India borrowed: Fundamental Rights, judicial review, Preamble idea, impeachment of President, Vice-President as chairman of upper house.
## French Revolution (1789)
- Causes: absolute monarchy (Louis XVI), three estates with privileges, financial bankruptcy, bread prices, Enlightenment ideas.
@chain Estates-General May 1789 -> Tennis Court Oath -> Bastille 14 July -> Declaration of Rights of Man -> Republic 1792 -> Reign of Terror (Robespierre) -> Directory -> Napoleon 1799 :: French Revolution in one line
- Legacy: **Liberty, Equality, Fraternity** (in our Preamble), end of feudalism, nationalism, the metric system, Napoleonic Code.
## Nationalism and Unifications
- **Germany** (1871): Bismarck's "blood and iron"; wars with Denmark, Austria, France; Prussia led; Zollverein (customs union).
- **Italy** (1861-70): Mazzini (soul), Cavour (brain), Garibaldi (sword), Victor Emmanuel II.
## Imperialism and Colonialism
- **Colonialism** = direct political control and settlement; **imperialism** = wider domination (economic, political). Causes: Industrial Revolution needs, nationalism, "white man's burden". **Scramble for Africa** - Berlin Conference 1884-85. Opium Wars in China.
## First World War (1914-18)
- Causes (MAIN): **M**ilitarism, **A**lliances (Triple Alliance vs Triple Entente), **I**mperialism, **N**ationalism; trigger - assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo.
- Results: four empires fell (German, Austro-Hungarian, Ottoman, Russian); **Treaty of Versailles 1919** (war guilt, reparations on Germany); League of Nations; seeds of WW2. Impact on India: heavy taxes, recruitment, price rise -> Rowlatt, Khilafat, Non-Cooperation.
## Russian Revolution (1917)
- Causes: Tsarist autocracy, peasant misery, WW1 defeats. February Revolution removed the Tsar; **October Revolution** - Bolsheviks under Lenin ("Peace, Land, Bread"). USSR formed 1922. Stalin: Five-Year Plans, collectivisation.
- Impact: spread of socialism, inspired planning in India and anti-colonial movements.
## Inter-war Period, Fascism and Second World War
- **Great Depression 1929**: Wall Street crash, mass unemployment; New Deal (Roosevelt). Rise of **fascism** (Mussolini, 1922) and **Nazism** (Hitler, 1933): ultra-nationalism, one leader, anti-communism, racial hatred.
- WW2 (1939-45): causes - Versailles humiliation, appeasement, failure of the League, aggression. Began with invasion of Poland; ended with Hiroshima and Nagasaki (Aug 1945).
- Results: **United Nations (24 Oct 1945)**, Bretton Woods (IMF, World Bank), decolonisation, two superpowers.
## Cold War and After
| Feature | USA bloc | USSR bloc |
|---|---|---|
| Ideology | capitalism, liberal democracy | communism |
| Military | NATO 1949 | Warsaw Pact 1955 |
| Economic | Marshall Plan | COMECON |
- Flashpoints: Berlin blockade, Korean War, Cuban Missile Crisis 1962, Vietnam. **NAM** (Belgrade 1961: Nehru, Nasser, Tito, Sukarno, Nkrumah) kept a third path.
- **Decolonisation**: Asia first (India 1947, Indonesia 1949), Africa (Ghana 1957; "Year of Africa" 1960). Causes: weak Europe, nationalism, UN, superpower pressure.
- **Disintegration of USSR 1991**: economic stagnation, arms race, Gorbachev's glasnost and perestroika, nationalism of republics. Result: unipolar world, then multipolarity (rise of China, India).
| Ideology | Core idea |
|---|---|
| Capitalism | private ownership, market, profit |
| Socialism | social ownership, equality, state role |
| Communism | classless, stateless society (Marx) |
| Fascism | supreme nation-state, dictatorship |
## Indian Architecture
### Buddhist and rock-cut
- **Stupa** parts: anda (dome), harmika, chhatra, vedika (railing), torana (gateway). Sanchi, Bharhut, Amaravati.
- **Chaitya** = prayer hall; **Vihara** = monastery. Ajanta (Buddhist, paintings), Ellora (Buddhist, Hindu, Jain; Kailasa temple), Elephanta (Trimurti), Karle, Bhaja, Barabar.
### Temple styles
| Feature | Nagara (north) | Dravida (south) | Vesara (Deccan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tower | curvilinear **shikhara** | pyramidal **vimana** | mix |
| Gateway | modest | huge **gopuram** | - |
| Boundary | usually none | compound wall | - |
| Tank | absent | present | - |
| Examples | Khajuraho (Chandela), Konark Sun Temple, Lingaraja, Dilwara | Brihadeshwara, Meenakshi, Shore Temple | Pattadakal, Hoysala temples (Belur, Halebidu - star-shaped) |
- Basic parts: garbhagriha (sanctum), mandapa (hall), shikhara, vahana, amalaka and kalasha. Odisha sub-style: rekha deul, jagamohana.
### Indo-Islamic and colonial
- Features: arch and dome (arcuate) instead of beam (trabeate), minarets, calligraphy, arabesque, charbagh gardens, pietra dura.
- Sultanate: Qutub Minar, Alai Darwaza. Mughal: Humayun's Tomb (first garden tomb), Fatehpur Sikri (Buland Darwaza), Taj Mahal, Red Fort. Regional: Gol Gumbaz, Charminar.
- Colonial: Gothic (Victoria Terminus/CSMT), Indo-Saracenic (Madras High Court), Neo-classical; Lutyens' Delhi.
## Sculpture and Painting
- Sculpture: Harappan bronze dancing girl -> Mauryan polish -> Gandhara, Mathura, Amaravati -> Gupta (Sarnath Buddha) -> Chola bronzes.
- **Prehistoric**: Bhimbetka. **Murals**: Ajanta (Bodhisattva Padmapani), Bagh, Sittanavasal (Jain), Lepakshi.
| Painting school | Features |
|---|---|
| Mughal | Persian influence, realism, portraits, court scenes; peak under Jahangir (Mansur, Abul Hasan) |
| Rajput / Rajasthani | Krishna themes, bold colours; Mewar, Bundi, Kishangarh (Bani Thani) |
| Pahari | Basohli, Kangra - delicate, lyrical |
| Folk | Madhubani (Bihar), Warli (Maharashtra), Pattachitra (Odisha), Kalamkari (Andhra), Gond, Phad, Thangka |
| Modern | Raja Ravi Varma; Bengal School (Abanindranath Tagore); Amrita Sher-Gil |
## Performing Arts
| Classical dance | State | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Bharatanatyam | Tamil Nadu | oldest, devadasi tradition, ekaharya |
| Kathak | North India | storytellers, spins, Lucknow and Jaipur gharanas |
| Kathakali | Kerala | dance-drama, heavy make-up, male tradition |
| Mohiniyattam | Kerala | graceful, solo women |
| Kuchipudi | Andhra Pradesh | dance on brass plate |
| Odissi | Odisha | tribhanga posture |
| Manipuri | Manipur | Ras Lila |
| Sattriya | Assam | Sankardeva, monasteries |
- Eight classical dances are recognised by Sangeet Natak Akademi (Ministry of Culture also lists Chhau). Source text: **Natya Shastra** of Bharata.
- **Music**: Hindustani (north; Persian influence; gharanas; dhrupad, khayal, thumri; Tansen, Amir Khusrau) vs Carnatic (south; kriti-based; Trinity - Tyagaraja, Dikshitar, Syama Sastri; Purandara Dasa as father).
- Folk dances: Bhangra, Garba, Bihu, Ghoomar, Lavani, Chhau, Yakshagana (theatre), Kalbelia. Theatre: Jatra, Nautanki, Tamasha, Koodiyattam.
## Literature, Philosophy and Religion
- Sanskrit: Vedas, epics, Panini's Ashtadhyayi, Kalidasa, Kalhana's Rajatarangini. Pali: Tripitaka, Jatakas. Tamil: Sangam, Tirukkural (Thiruvalluvar). Persian: Akbarnama (Abul Fazl). Modern: Tagore (Nobel 1913), Premchand, Subramania Bharati.
- **Six schools** (Shad-darshana): Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, Vedanta. Heterodox: Buddhism, Jainism, Charvaka (materialism).
- **Classical languages**: Tamil, Sanskrit, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, and from 2024 Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali (total 11).
## Heritage and Institutions
- **UNESCO World Heritage Sites** in India: 40+ (cultural, natural, and one mixed - Khangchendzonga). Recent additions include Dholavira and Ramappa (2021), Santiniketan and Hoysala ensembles (2023), Moidams of Assam (2024), Maratha Military Landscapes (2025). Always check the latest count.
- **Intangible Cultural Heritage**: Vedic chanting, Ramlila, Koodiyattam, Yoga, Kumbh Mela, Durga Puja, Garba.
- Institutions: ASI (1861, Cunningham), Sangeet Natak, Sahitya and Lalit Kala Akademis, INTACH, National Museum. GI tags protect crafts (Geographical Indications Act 1999).
## Prelims MCQs with Explanations
Q: "Shikhara" and "gopuram" are respectively associated with (a) Dravida, Nagara (b) Nagara, Dravida (c) both Nagara (d) both Vesara
A: (b).
Q: Which pair is wrong? (a) Sattriya - Assam (b) Mohiniyattam - Kerala (c) Kuchipudi - Karnataka (d) Odissi - Odisha
A: (c). Kuchipudi is from Andhra Pradesh.
Q: Kailasa temple at Ellora was built by (a) Pallavas (b) Rashtrakutas (c) Cholas (d) Chalukyas
A: (b), under Krishna I; carved top-down from a single rock.
Q: Treaty of Versailles is linked to (a) Napoleonic wars (b) WW1 (c) WW2 (d) Cold War
A: (b).
Q: Bani Thani belongs to which school? (a) Kangra (b) Kishangarh (c) Mughal (d) Madhubani
A: (b).
## Mains Questions with Model Structures
Q: Why did the Industrial Revolution first occur in England? Discuss its effects on India.
Structure: factors (resources, capital, colonies, technology, stability) -> effects on India (deindustrialisation, raw material supplier, railways, drain). Conclude with link to nationalism.
Q: To what extent was the Treaty of Versailles responsible for the Second World War?
Structure: harsh terms -> humiliation -> Hitler's rise; but also Depression, appeasement, League's failure. Balanced conclusion.
Q: Chola architecture represents the high watermark of temple building. Discuss.
Structure: Dravida features, Brihadeshwara (vimana 60 m, single-stone cupola), bronzes, temple as economic and social centre.
Q: Safeguarding Indian art heritage is the need of the moment. Discuss.
Structure: threats (theft, neglect, urbanisation, climate) -> measures (ASI, AMASR Act, digitisation, community role, tourism) -> way forward.
